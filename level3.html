LEVEL 3
🔍 Environmental Word Search Challenge
  1. Find the word shown at the top of the screen.
  2. You only have 15 seconds to find each word.
  3. Only ONE word will appear at a time.
  4. After finding the word, its definition will be displayed.
  5. If the timer reaches zero, the answer and definition will be shown automatically.
  6. The next word will appear after the definition.
  7. Complete all 5 words to receive the passcode for the next level.
TIP: Words may be hidden horizontally, vertically, diagonally, or backwards. Work together and look carefully!

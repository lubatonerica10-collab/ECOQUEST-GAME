📚 What You've Learned

✅ Good Benefits & Their Meanings

Clean Air

Fresh, unpolluted air helps us breathe easily and keeps our lungs strong and healthy.

Safe Drinking Water

Clean, pure water prevents sickness and keeps our bodies hydrated and functioning well.

Healthy Soil

Rich, nutrient-filled soil grows healthy food so we stay strong and energetic.

Lower Disease Risk

A clean environment means fewer germs, toxins, and diseases spread among people.

Biodiversity

Many different plants and animals keep nature balanced and support all life on Earth.

Fresh Food Supply

Healthy ecosystems grow plenty of nutritious food so no one goes hungry.

Cooler Temperatures

Trees and green spaces cool the air, making our surroundings comfortable and safe.

Mental Wellbeing

Being in nature calms our minds, reduces stress, and makes us feel happier.

⚠️ Bad Effects & Their Meanings

Air Pollution

Dirty, smoky air from cars and factories causes breathing problems, allergies, and lung disease.

Water Contamination

Dirty water carries germs and chemicals that make us very sick and unsafe to drink.

Soil Erosion

When soil washes away, nothing grows well and we lose our food supply.

Spread of Diseases

A dirty environment helps germs and viruses spread faster and make more people ill.

Deforestation

Cutting down too many trees removes homes for wildlife, reduces clean air, and warms the planet.

Waste Accumulation

Too much trash piles up, poisons the land, and harms animals and people.

Global Warming

Earth gets too hot, ice melts, seas rise, and weather becomes dangerous and extreme.

Loss of Wildlife

When animals disappear, nature becomes unbalanced and affects all life including us.

LEVEL 2 - JIGSAW PUZZLE

Game Instructions

  • There are 5 puzzle levels.
  • You have 2 minutes for each puzzle.
  • Each puzzle is worth 20 points.
  • If time runs out, you automatically continue but receive 10 points for that puzzle.
  • The highest possible score is 100 points.
  • You must earn 80 points or higher to receive the Level 3 passkey.
  • Work together with your group and finish as quickly as possible.
